# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to make participation in this project respectful, constructive, and welcoming for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or perspective.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, specific, and open to good-faith feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas, evidence, and code rather than people.
- Protect others’ privacy: never share transcripts, secrets, personal paths, customer data, or
  private repository details without clear permission.
- Respect project safety boundaries, including human review before any external sending or local
  application of a proposal.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sustained disruption, publication of private
information, or attempts to pressure someone into sharing sensitive data are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through the maintainer’s private contact route listed in the repository
profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove,
edit, or reject contributions and take other appropriate action to protect participants.
